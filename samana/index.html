Prices at SAMANA Ocean Crest begin at AED 2,149,000, varying according to layout, floor level, private pool inclusions, and orientation towards Dubai Islands' waterfront.
SAMANA Ocean Crest is situated on Dubai Islands, Dubai — an exclusive archipelago of waterfront luxury offering resort-style living with marinas and beaches, while keeping rapid connectivity to the city center:
  • Dubai Islands Marina & Dubai Frame — 10 to 15 minutes
  • Dubai International Airport, DIFC, Downtown Dubai & Dubai Mall — 15 to 20 minutes
