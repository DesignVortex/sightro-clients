FAQ

Shahrukhz Residences: questions buyers ask first

Answers reflect the developer’s launch information. Prices and terms can change.

What is Shahrukhz Residences?

Shahrukhz Residences is a 60-floor waterfront tower of fully furnished homes by Danube Properties in Dubai Maritime City. It is the first residential launch under the Shahrukhz brand, following Shahrukhz by Danube, the 55-storey office tower on Sheikh Zayed Road.

What is the starting price of Shahrukhz Residences?

Prices start at AED 1.7M for a studio. One-bedroom homes start at AED 2.3M, the 2 BHK Premium at AED 4.25M and the 3 BHK at AED 6.5M. Penthouse pricing is on request. Prices are as issued by the developer and can change.

What is the payment plan?

A 30/70 plan: 10% down payment within 21 days of booking, 10% at 2 months, 5% at 10 months, 5% at 18 months, and the remaining 70% on handover in December 2029.

How much is the EOI for Shahrukhz Residences?

The Expression of Interest is AED 50,000 for a studio or 1 BHK and AED 100,000 for a 2 BHK and above. The developer gives priority allocation of units to buyers who pay the 10% down payment.

When is handover?

Handover is scheduled for December 2029 (Q4 2029).

Which unit types are available?

Studio, Flex Studio, 1 BHK, Flex 1 BHK, Flex Plus 1 BHK, King’s 1 BHK, 2 BHK Premium, King’s 2 BHK and 3 BHK, each with a Skyline or Panoramic sea view, plus exclusive penthouses designed by Gauri Khan.

Are the apartments furnished?

Yes. The residences are sold fully furnished, with Italian kitchen and bathroom fittings.

Where is Shahrukhz Residences located?

In Dubai Maritime City, the waterfront peninsula between Port Rashid and Dubai Drydocks, next to Bur Dubai. The developer states about 10 minutes to Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall, 8 minutes to Jumeirah Beach and 15 minutes to DXB International Airport.

What amenities does Shahrukhz Residences have?

More than 40 amenities across three decks: the Lifestyle Deck on the podium (infinity beach pool, kids’ water park, padel, basketball and badminton courts, cricket pitch, indoor cinema), the Wellness Deck on Level 33 (Infinity 33 pool, gym and a Longevity Lounge with cryotherapy, hyperbaric oxygen and red light therapy) and the Sky Deck on Level 60 with the King’s Club House.

Is there a yacht for residents?

Yes. The developer has announced a dedicated yacht reserved exclusively for Shahrukhz residents, with complimentary access. Booking terms will be set by the developer.

Can foreigners buy at Shahrukhz Residences?

Yes. Dubai Maritime City is a freehold area, so buyers of any nationality can own an apartment outright.

Is the EOI refundable?

Refund terms are set by the developer and confirmed in writing before you pay. Ask for them, and pay only into the developer’s account named in writing.

Is Shahrukhz Residences the same project as Shahrukhz by Danube on Sheikh Zayed Road?

No. Shahrukhz by Danube on Sheikh Zayed Road is a commercial office tower. Shahrukhz Residences is a separate residential project in Dubai Maritime City.
