Palm Central Private Residences

A Calm Called Home

At the heart of Palm Jebel Ali, Palm Central Private Residences offers contemporary, resort-style homes across three elegant buildings. Each residence is thoughtfully oriented to capture uninterrupted island views, embodying serene sophistication, natural beauty, and the timeless prestige of coastal living.

Situated between the fronds, its stepped form opens each home to uninterrupted views of Palm Jebel Ali, from sunrise to starlight. Each residence is an expression of serenity and connection, defined by open layout, natural textures, and a quiet prestige that reflects the essence of island life.
