About This Home

MASTERFULLY BUILT TWO-STORY HOME IN THE AVALON GATED COMMUNITY AT RIVERSTONE. Built by the highly acclaimed Taylor Morrison builder recognized for their high-quality workmanship and top-grade luxury amenities. Beautiful well-kept home available from the original owners. Home boasts 4 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, and a tandem 3-car garage. Features the elegant & functional Bevington Floor Plan. Dramatic entry with soaring high ceiling leads to a beautiful living room with huge windows for lots of natural light. Entryway leads to a study/office with elegant French doors and a dining room leading to a large chef-inspired kitchen. Two primary bedrooms, one on each floor. The primary suite showcases a spa-like bathroom that includes a HUGE walk-in closet. Media room adjacent to large game room. Nice amenities: water softener, outdoor connections gas/water for an outdoor kitchen, and sprinkler system. Top-rated Ft. Bend schools. Near retail stores and restaurants.
