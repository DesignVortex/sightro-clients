A masterclass in modern living by Taylor Morrison.

Situated in the exclusive gated community of Avalon at Riverstone, this masterfully built two-story residence showcases high-quality workmanship and top-grade luxury amenities. Available from the original owners, the home features the highly sought-after Bevington Floor Plan.

A dramatic entry with soaring ceilings leads to a light-filled living room featuring massive windows. Designed for entertaining, the main floor includes a dedicated study with French doors, an elegant dining room, and a massive chef-inspired kitchen.

The estate boasts two primary suites—one on each level. The main primary suite showcases a spa-like bathroom complete with a massive walk-in closet. The second level is an entertainer's dream, featuring an expansive game room adjacent to a dedicated media room. Outdoors, the property is pre-plumbed for a summer kitchen and is zoned to top-rated Fort Bend schools.
