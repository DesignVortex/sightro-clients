FAQ

Couture by Cavalli is an off-plan ultra-luxury residential development by DAMAC Properties, currently under active construction with anticipated handover scheduled for Q2, 2027.

The project offers exclusive 3, 4, and 5-bedroom luxury apartments branded by Roberto Cavalli, along with a limited collection of ultra-exclusive 6-bedroom penthouses with private pools.

Residents enjoy prime canal-front promenades, direct access to Safa Park's lush green landscapes, fine dining along the Dubai Water Canal, luxury retail, and tranquil waterfront running and cycling tracks.

The Dubai Mall (approx. 10 minutes drive), City Walk (approx. 8 minutes drive), and Mall of the Emirates (approx. 15 minutes drive).

Medcare Hospital Al Safa, Emirates Hospital Clinic, and Aster Clinic Business Bay are all within 5 to 10 minutes drive.

Four Seasons Resort Dubai at Jumeirah Beach, The St. Regis Downtown Dubai, and Habtoor Palace Dubai are all within 5 to 8 minutes drive.

JSS Private School, Horizon English School, and Dubai International School are all within 7 to 12 minutes drive.

Dubai International Airport (DXB) is approximately 15-20 minutes drive away, offering seamless global connectivity.
