Welcome to 10 Church Street, where true small-town living meets the best of a thoughtful modern remodel. Built in 1940, this charming old house has all the character you hope for in a vintage home - paired with the updates and comforts you actually want to live with.<br><br> Tucked away on a quiet dead-end street, there's virtually no traffic to speak of. Just peace, quiet, and the kind of neighborhood where a leisurely walk can become part of your daily routine.<br><br> And speaking of walking... you really can leave the car at home.<br><br> Downtown Round Hill is right around the corner, with Tammy's Diner for breakfast, the post office and the park, Elsass Antiques for treasure hunting, and Round Hill Farm to Trail Company for sandwiches, yummy baked goods and trail equipment for hiking the AT. And when it's time for a special celebration, C'est Bon is close enough to walk to. In other words, your car may start wondering if it still has a job.<br><br> Inside, the home offers over 2200 finished square feet, including 3 bedrooms and 2 full baths, with a layout that balances everyday comfort with plenty of flexibility.<br><br> The main level includes two bedrooms, a remodeled full bath, convenient laundry, and a welcoming living room with a fireplace - the perfect spot for cozy evenings. The remodeled kitchen brings the old house into the present with quartz countertops, gray cabinetry, and stainless-steel appliances, while still fitting beautifully with the home's original character.<br><br> Head upstairs and discover a cozy primary suite, complete with its own bedroom and bath - a private retreat tucked beneath the roofline.<br><br> The lower level adds even more living space, with a recreation room, office, and plenty of unfinished space for storage, hobbies, a workshop, or whatever else your imagination comes up with.<br><br> Outside, the flat, fully fenced quarter-acre yard is a real bonus. There's room for gardening, play, pets, entertaining, or simply enjoying a little fresh air without having to mow a football field.<br><br> It's a home with history, personality, and charm - but without requiring you to live like it's 1940. And did you hear - ''no HOA?'' That usually brings a smile.<br><br> 10 Church Street offers the rare combination of character, quality updates, a wonderfully usable yard, and a walkable small-town lifestyle. Come for the charm. Stay for the convenience. And enjoy the fact that sometimes, the best way to get around town is simply to put on your shoes and head out the door.
